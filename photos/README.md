# Photos for review

Drop scan photos here (from your phone) and ask Claude to look at them.

## Getting photos off the phone
The app saves every scan to **Documents/FloraIQ/** on your device:
- `2026-07-22_1432_Monstera-deliciosa.jpg` — the photo
- `scan-log.json` — what the AI identified, confidence, GPS, date

Copy that folder over with a USB cable (Phone → Internal storage → Documents → FloraIQ)
and paste the files into this folder.

Then say: *"look at the photos in photos/ and tell me why the ID was wrong"*
and I can review the images and the log together.
